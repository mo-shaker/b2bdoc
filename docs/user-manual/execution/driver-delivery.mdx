**Role**
Driver

**Navigate to**
`SCOP → My Work`

## Purpose

Everything a driver does, in the order they do it. The screens are built for a phone and
carry only what is needed at the door.

## Your whole day

```text
My Trips
   │  Depart
   ▼
My Stops  ── for each stop, in sequence ──┐
   │  I have arrived                      │
   │  Start unloading                     │
   │  Delivered  ── or ──  Could not deliver
   └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Navigate to** `SCOP → My Work → My Trips`

**What you see**
Cards, one per trip, showing the vehicle, the date and how many stops.

The list is filtered to trips that are **Released** or later. A plan still being argued over
is not your concern.

hiding a menu.

## Step 2 — Depart

**Before you start**
The trip must be **Ready to Depart** — which means the warehouse has finished loading.

**What to do**
Open the trip and press **Depart**.

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/26-driver-trip-ready.png"
  alt="The driver's trip form in state Ready to Depart, showing the Depart button."
  caption="The driver's trip form. One button, and only when it is legal."
  device="mobile"
/>

**What SCOP does**

| Effect | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Records the departure time | |
<Screenshot
  src="user-manual/D03-driver-trip-form.png"
  alt="The driver's simplified trip form on a phone-width screen, showing the trip status and its action button."
  caption="The driver's trip form. At phone width the lifecycle buttons collapse into a single Action menu."

| The trip becomes **In Progress** | |
| Everything downstream follows | Shipments and demands move into execution on the system's behalf |

:::note[If Depart is not there]

The trip is not **Ready to Depart** yet. A loading task is still open — ask the warehouse.

### I have arrived

**When** You are at the location.
**What SCOP does** Records the arrival time and moves the stop to **Arrived**.

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/30-driver-stop-arrived.png"
  alt="The driver's stop form in status Arrived, showing the Start unloading button."
  caption="Arrived. Arrival time is recorded and feeds the on-time measure."
  device="mobile"
/>

Arrival time is what [OTIF](../reporting/otif.mdx) measures on-time against, so press it
when you actually arrive.

### Start unloading

**When** You begin handing over the goods.
**What SCOP does** Records the service start and moves the stop to **Servicing**.

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/31-driver-stop-unloading.png"
  alt="The driver's stop form in status Servicing, showing the Delivered and Could not deliver buttons."
  caption="Servicing. From here the stop ends one of two ways."
  device="mobile"
/>

### Delivered

**When** The goods are handed over and the warehouse has validated the transfer.

**What SCOP does**

| Effect | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Records the departure time and the service duration | |
| Derives the **outcome** from the quantities that actually moved | |
| Moves the stop to **Completed** | |
| Closes the trip if this was the last stop | |

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/36-driver-stop-delivered.png"
  alt="The driver's stop form in status Completed."
  caption="Completed. The outcome came from the quantities, not from a choice."
  device="mobile"
/>

### Could not deliver

**When** The delivery genuinely did not happen.

**A reason is required.** See [Failed Delivery](./failed-delivery.mdx).

## The Goods tab

Shows what should be delivered, and what actually was.

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/33-driver-goods-quantities.png"
  alt="The driver's Goods tab showing products with planned and actual quantities."
  caption="Planned against actual. The actual column is read from the Odoo transfer."
  device="mobile"
/>

:::warning[You never type the delivered quantity]

The actual column is read from the **Odoo transfer the warehouse validates**. There is no
field for you to fill in.

Your job is to report what happened. The warehouse records it.

→ [Odoo Delivery Validation](./odoo-delivery-validation.mdx)

:::

### If the quantities are zero

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/39-awaiting-odoo-validation.png"
  alt="A message explaining that no delivered quantity has been recorded yet and the stop cannot be closed."
  caption="Not a failure. The transfer has not been validated yet."
  device="mobile"
/>

:::danger[Zero quantity is not a failed delivery]

If the transfer has not been validated yet, quantities show zero. **Do not record a failure
for this reason.**

Wait for the warehouse to validate, then press **Delivered**. A failure recorded here would
have to be unpicked from every service measure afterwards.

:::

## Working on a phone

The driver screens are built for phone width. The cards are the primary view; the form opens
from a card.

Practical points:

| Point | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Press each button **when the thing happens** | The times are measurements, not paperwork |
| A double tap is harmless | A closed stop stays closed and is not re-stamped |
| Coordinates need `https` | On plain `http` they record as unavailable, with a reason. Signature and photographs still work |
| You cannot reach anything else | By design — see below |

## What you cannot see

| Not available to you | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Other drivers' trips | You see only trips assigned to you |
| Demands, shipments, plans | Not part of your work |
| Warehouse readiness, loading tasks | Not part of your work |
| Inventory transfers | You would be able to read every movement in the company |
| Governance and reports | Not part of your work |

→ [Driver Security Model](./driver-security-model.mdx)

## Verify

| Check | Where |
| --- | --- |
| The trip departed | The trip card shows **In Progress** |
| Each stop was closed | The stop cards show **Completed** |
| Quantities came back | The **Goods** tab |
| Proof was captured | The **Proof of delivery** tab |
| The trip closed | The trip card |

## If it does not work

| Symptom | Cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **My Trips** is empty | No trip assigned to you, or none released | Ask dispatch |
| **Depart** is missing | The trip is not **Ready to Depart** | A loading task is open — ask the warehouse |
| **Delivered** is refused | The transfer is not validated | Wait, then try again |
| Quantities are zero | Same cause | **Do not** record a failure |
| **Could not deliver** is refused | No reason chosen | Choose one |
| Coordinates were not captured | The site is on plain `http` | Nothing — it is recorded as unavailable, with a reason |
| A stop you already closed reopened | It did not. A closed stop stays closed | — |
