## Loading tasks missing

**How to verify** `Operations → Loading Tasks`, filtered to the trip; or the trip's
**Loading** tab.

| Cause | Verify | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The trip is not released | The trip's status | Release the plan, or the trip |
| The trip has no shipments | The trip's **Stops** tab | An empty trip has nothing to load |
| Release did not complete | The trip's chatter | Read the message |

:::info[One loading task per shipment on the trip]

A trip carrying three shipments should have three tasks. A count that does not match the
shipment count is the signal.

:::

## Trip cannot depart

### 1 · A loading task is still open

**How to verify** `Operations → Loading Tasks` — any task not **Done**.
**Distinguishing signal** The trip is stuck in **Loading**, not **Ready to Depart**.
**Correct action** Settle every task with **Done**.

:::info[This is the departure gate working]

An open loading task holds the vehicle. That is the design, not a fault.

:::

### 2 · Loading was never started

**How to verify** The trip is in **Released**, not **Loading**.
**Correct action** Press **Start** on the first loading task.

### 3 · The person pressing Depart is not entitled

**How to verify** The refusal names authorisation.
**Cause** Only the **assigned driver** and a **Planner / Dispatcher** may depart a trip.
**Correct action** One of those two departs it.

:::note[A warehouse user cannot depart a vehicle]

Even one who happens to hold write access on trips. Reachability is not authorisation.

:::

## Driver sees no trips

Work down in order.

| # | Cause | Verify | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | No trip is assigned to them | The trip's **Driver** field | Assign one |
| 2 | The trip is not released | The trip's status | Release the plan |
| 3 | **The employee is not linked to a user account** | Odoo Employees | Link it |
| 4 | The employee is not flagged **Is Driver** | Odoo Employees | Flag it |
| 5 | The user does not hold the **Driver** SCOP role | The user's SCOP category | Assign it |

:::danger[Cause 3 is the one that is missed]

The record rules match on the **employee's linked user account**, not the employee alone.

An employee flagged as a driver but not linked to a user can be assigned a trip that **nobody
will ever see**.

:::

→ [Drivers](../configuration/drivers.mdx)

## Driver sees other people's work

**Cause** The user holds `SCOP User` in addition to `Driver`.
**Correct action** Remove `SCOP User`.

:::warning[Driver deliberately does not inherit SCOP User]

`SCOP User` grants read access across the whole operation. Adding it to a driver silently
undoes the driver security model.

:::

→ [Driver Security Model](../execution/driver-security-model.mdx)

## Actual quantity is zero

**How to verify** The stop's **Quantities** tab, or the driver's **Goods** tab.
**Cause** **The Odoo transfer has not been validated.**

:::danger[This is not a failed delivery]

The goods may be at the customer and the paperwork simply behind.

**Do not press Could not deliver.** A failure recorded here has to be unpicked from every
service measure afterwards.

:::

**Correct action** The warehouse validates the transfer in Odoo with the quantity that
actually moved. SCOP reads it back. Then close the stop.

→ [Odoo Delivery Validation](../execution/odoo-delivery-validation.mdx)

## Stop cannot complete

### 1 · No delivered quantity recorded yet

**How to verify** The refusal says so explicitly, and tells you to wait.
**Correct action** Wait for validation. Then press **Delivered**.

### 2 · Something was short and no reason was given

**How to verify** The refusal reads *"did not deliver everything planned, so it needs a
reason"*.
**Cause** `BR-EXE-001`.
**Correct action** Choose one of the seven stop-outcome reasons.

### 3 · The stop is already closed

**How to verify** Its status is Completed, Failed or Skipped.
**Correct action** None. A closed stop stays closed, and pressing again does nothing.

:::note[This is deliberate]

A double tap, or a reconnecting phone replaying the call, would otherwise re-stamp the
departure time and silently rewrite a measurement of something that happened once.

:::

---

## Could not deliver is refused

**Cause** No **outcome reason** was chosen.
**Correct action** Choose one. `BR-EXE-001` requires it, and you are asked at the door because
that is when you can answer.

---

## Trip cannot complete

| Cause | Verify | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A stop has no outcome | The trip's **Stops** tab | Close every stop |
| A failed or partial stop has no reason | `BR-EXE-001` | Give it one |
| The trip is not **In Progress** or **Exception** | The status bar | — |

---

## Trip closed as Partially Completed unexpectedly

**Cause** At least one stop's outcome was not **Completed** — it was partial, failed or
skipped.
**How to verify** The trip's **Stops** tab, **Outcome** column.
**Correct action** None. It is an accurate record. The requirement continues in the follow-up
demands.

---

## Demands still In Execution after the trip closed

**Cause** Their Odoo transfers were not validated.
**Correct action** Validate them. SCOP reads the quantities back and the demands close.

---

## No coordinates on proof of delivery

**How to verify** **Has Location** is false; **Capture Failed Reason** states why.
**Cause** Browsers refuse to give a page a location on a plain `http` origin.
**Correct action** Deploy on `https`. Signature and photographs are unaffected.

:::note[The failure is recorded honestly]

Latitude zero, longitude zero is a real place. Recording it without a reason would put a
plausible-looking coordinate into the evidence chain, so SCOP records the reason instead.

:::

---

## A load is stuck

| State | Cause | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Proposed**, will not confirm | `BR-RTE-001` — capacity breached | Fix the trip's capacity |
| **Confirmed**, will not stage | Not every shipment line is staged | Stage them |
| **Staged**, will not load | Actual loaded quantities are not recorded | Complete the loading tasks |

---

## A trip is stuck in Exception

**Cause** **Resume** requires the originating exception to be **resolved** or **closed**.
**How to verify** `Operations → Exceptions` — find the exception on this trip.
**Correct action** Work the exception to resolution, then **Resume**. Or **Complete** the trip
as partially completed, once every stop has an outcome.

→ [Exception Management](../exceptions/exception-management.mdx)
