:::info[Start with the Evidence field]

Readiness always states its reasoning in words. `Operations → Warehouse Readiness` → the
**Evidence** field answers most of this chapter before you read it.

:::

---

## Readiness stays Blocked

### 1 · Ownership is unresolved

**How to verify** Evidence reads *"Ownership is unresolved on N line(s): …"* and names the
products.
**Correct action** Resolve the ownership on the underlying demand.

→ [Correcting Ownership](../ownership/correcting-ownership.mdx)

### 2 · Every transfer behind the shipment is cancelled

**How to verify** Evidence reads *"Every transfer behind this shipment is cancelled."*
**Correct action** The movement is not happening. Cancel the shipment.

:::note[Blocked is tested before any quantity]

A shipment whose owner is unresolved is not "partially ready" — it is not plannable at all.
Testing ownership first stops a well-prepared pallet masking a legal problem.

:::

:::warning[An override does not resolve ownership]

Overriding readiness to **Ready** makes the shipment plannable. It does **not** resolve
ownership, and `BR-INV-002` will still refuse to validate the demand.

:::

---

## Readiness stays Not Ready

### 1 · Nothing is reserved in Odoo

**How to verify** Evidence reads *"Nothing is reserved or staged yet."*
**Correct action** Check availability and reserve the stock on the Odoo transfer, then press
**Refresh Readiness**.

### 2 · The shipment has no lines

**How to verify** Evidence reads *"The shipment has no lines."*
**Correct action** Investigate. A shipment with no lines usually means the demand it formed
from had none.

---

## Readiness stays Partially Ready

**How to verify** Evidence reads *"N of M units are reserved or staged."*

**Correct action** Reserve or stage the remainder.

:::warning[This is the state that quietly stalls a demand]

**Partially Ready** lets a demand become **Eligible for Planning** but does **not** make its
shipment plannable.

The visible symptom is a demand sitting at eligible forever while nobody notices why.

:::

---

## Readiness went backwards

**How to verify** The state moved from **Ready** to **Conditionally Ready**, or from
**Ready** to **Created** on the shipment.
**Cause** A reservation was removed, staging was reversed, or a transfer was cancelled.
**Correct action** **None — this is correct.** Readiness is computed from current evidence,
and evidence changes.

---

## Stage Everything does nothing visible

| Cause | Verify | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Readiness was not recomputed on screen | — | Press **Refresh Readiness** |
| The lines were already staged | The **Lines** tab | None |
| Readiness is **Blocked** | The **Evidence** field | Ownership is blocking. Fix that first |
| You are not a Warehouse user | The button is absent | Ask a Warehouse user |
| The shipment is past **Ready** | The button is absent | Expected |

---

## Staging is rejected

**Cause** You cannot stage more than the shipment asks for.
**Correct action** Stage the shipment quantity. If more physically exists, that is an Odoo
quantity question.

---

## Staged % shows an absurd number

**How to verify** The column shows `10000%` where it means `100%`.
**Cause** A known display defect. The **stored** value is correct; only the column is scaled
twice.
**Correct action** Read it as a hundredth, or use **Readiness**, which is computed from the
same evidence and is unaffected. Plan Coverage is unaffected.

→ [Release 1 Scope](../about/release-1-scope.mdx)

---

## No shipment formed after Mark Eligible

This is rare, because formation and eligibility succeed or fail together.

| Cause | Verify | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The demand has no lines | The **Lines** tab | A demand with no lines forms no shipment |
| The demand is not actually eligible | The status bar | Press **Mark Eligible** |
| Formation failed | The chatter on the demand | If formation cannot succeed, eligibility does not either. Read the message |

:::note[Formation is not allowed to fail quietly]

A demand at **Eligible for Planning** with no shipment is a state the warehouse cannot stage
against and the planner cannot plan. So if formation fails, the demand does not become
eligible either.

:::

## Two shipments where one was expected

Consolidation requires **all five** of these to match:

| # | Must match |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Origin node |
| 2 | Destination node |
| 3 | Shipment type |
| 4 | Company |
| 5 | **Required date** |

**How to verify** Compare the two demands across all five.
**Correct action** Usually none — a difference is a legitimate reason not to consolidate. If
the dates differ by mistake, correct the date on the transfer and regenerate.

### The sixth reason

The first shipment was already **planned** onto a trip, so it could not be joined.

:::info[A planned shipment is never joined]

Its capacity was sized when the plan was approved. Adding a pallet afterwards would break the
figure the plan was approved on.

:::

---

## One shipment where two were expected

That is consolidation working. Check the **Lines** tab — both demands are named, and each
line traces to its own demand and transfer.

If they genuinely must move separately, give them different required dates.

→ [Formation and Consolidation](../shipments/formation-and-consolidation.mdx)

---

## Two stops where one was expected

Requirements share a physical visit only when **all four** match:

| # | Must match |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The planning node |
| 2 | The activity — a pickup is not a delivery |
| 3 | The required date |
| 4 | The effective time window — a commitment outranks opening hours |

Any difference correctly keeps them apart.

→ [The Physical Visit](../shipments/the-physical-visit.mdx)
