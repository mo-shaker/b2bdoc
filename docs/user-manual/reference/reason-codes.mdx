## Why SCOP uses a controlled list

Wherever SCOP asks *why*, it offers a list rather than a free-text box.

:::info[A controlled vocabulary is what makes "why do we keep missing deliveries" answerable]

A hundred free-text comments cannot be grouped, counted or charted. Fifty-one controlled
reasons can — and every report that groups by reason exists because of this decision.

:::

## Choosing a reason honestly

Three of the six categories are chosen by a **person**: override, stop outcome and next
action. Those three carry all the analytical value, and all the risk.

:::warning[The convenient reason and the true reason are rarely the same]

| Chosen | True | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Local route knowledge* | *Incorrect master data* | A fixable data problem is hidden behind an unfixable one |
| *Customer location closed* | *Ran out of time on the route* | A routing problem is recorded as a customer problem |
| *Operational information unavailable to the engine* | *Unexpected warehouse condition* | A warehouse issue never reaches the warehouse |

A planner who always chooses the same reason produces a report that says nothing.

:::

The reverse is also true, and it is the point: a reason recorded honestly forty times
becomes a named, counted problem that somebody can actually fix.

## Where each category is used

| Category | Screen | Required? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unplanned Demand | `Planning → Unplanned Demand` | Set by the system |
| Override | Plan **Modify**; readiness **Apply Override** | **Yes** |
| Exception Root Cause | The exception form | No, but analytically essential |
| Stop Outcome | Driver **Could not deliver** / **Skipped** | **Yes** — `BR-EXE-001` |
| Next Action | Unplanned demand; demand **Reject** | Suggested; required on reject |
| Return Disposition | Return demand **Set Disposition** | **Yes** |

## Adding or changing reasons

`SCOP → Governance → Reason Codes`

Reasons can be deactivated and new ones added. Two cautions:

| Caution | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Deactivating a reason does not remove it from history | Past records keep the reason they were given |
| Adding near-duplicates destroys the value | Two reasons meaning the same thing split one signal into two weak ones |

Reasons marked as coming from the approved blueprint are the agreed vocabulary. Extend it
deliberately, not casually.
