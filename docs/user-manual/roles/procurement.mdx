## Your responsibility

You own **what a supplier promised us**, and you watch the inbound movements that promise
produces.

Procurement is a light role in Release 1. Most of your work stays in Odoo Purchase; SCOP
adds the commitment record and inbound visibility.

## Your menus

| Menu | What you use it for |
| --- | --- |
| `Master Data → Supplier Commitments` | What a supplier promised, and by when |
| `Operations → Demands` | Inbound demands generated from receipts |
| `Reporting → Operation Timeline` | Reconstruct one inbound operation |

Everything a SCOP User can read, you can read.

## Supplier commitments

A supplier commitment records what a supplier undertook to deliver, and when.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Partner | The supplier |
| Committed date or window | What they promised |
| Related requirement | What it covers |

It is the supplier-facing counterpart of a
[Service Commitment](../configuration/service-commitments.mdx), which records what *we*
promised a customer.

## Inbound demand

When Odoo creates a **receipt** whose operation type is inside pilot scope, SCOP generates
a demand with:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Demand Type** | **Inbound** |
| **Origin Type** | **Procurement Requirement** if a purchase order is behind it, otherwise **Supplier Collection** |

The demand then follows the same lifecycle as any other — see
[Demand Lifecycle](../demand/demand-lifecycle.mdx).

## What is not in Release 1

:::note[Procurement scope is deliberately narrow]

| Not available | Note |
| --- | --- |
| Purchase-order consignment terms | Consignment ownership comes from a **flagged partner**, not from order terms — see [Inventory Ownership](../ownership/inventory-ownership.mdx) |
| Supplier performance reporting | No supplier OTIF equivalent ships in Release 1 |
| Automated supplier alerts | None |

:::

## What you may not do

| Not permitted | Who does it |
| --- | --- |
| Validate or plan demands | Planner |
| Approve plans | Operations Manager |
| Flag a partner as a consignment owner | Administrator, via Onboarding |
