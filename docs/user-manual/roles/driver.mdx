## Your responsibility

Take the vehicle out, visit the stops in order, and **record what happened at each one**.

Your screen is deliberately simple. You see your own work and nothing else.

## Your menus

There are two, and no others.

| Menu | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `SCOP → My Work → My Trips` | Your trips, as cards |
| `SCOP → My Work → My Stops` | The stops on those trips, as cards |

Both show only trips that have been **released** — a plan still being argued over is not
your concern.

## Your day

```text
My Trips
   ↓  Depart
My Stops  (in sequence)
   ↓  I have arrived
   ↓  Start unloading
   ↓  Delivered      or      Could not deliver
```

| Button | When | What it records |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Depart** | The trip is **Ready to Depart** | Departure time. The trip goes **In Progress** |
| **I have arrived** | At the stop | Arrival time |
| **Start unloading** | Once you begin | Service start time |
| **Delivered** | The goods are handed over | The outcome, plus proof of delivery |
| **Could not deliver** | It did not happen | The outcome **and a reason** |

Full walkthrough with screens: [Driver Delivery](../execution/driver-delivery.mdx).

## The Goods tab

Your stop shows what should be delivered, and what actually was.

:::warning[You never type the delivered quantity]

The quantity comes from the **Odoo transfer the warehouse validates**. Your job is to report
what happened; the warehouse records it.

If quantities show zero, the transfer has not been validated yet. That is not a failed
delivery. See [Failed Delivery](../execution/failed-delivery.mdx).

:::

## Proof of delivery

On the **Proof of delivery** tab:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Signed by** | The name of the person who received the goods |
| **Signature** | Captured on screen |
| **Photographs** | Attach as many as needed |
| **Coordinates** | Captured automatically where the site is on `https` |

If coordinates cannot be captured, SCOP records **why** rather than pretending. That is
normal on a plain `http` deployment and is not your problem to fix.

:::info[This is not vehicle tracking]

Location is captured **once**, at the point of delivery. SCOP does not follow you between
stops, and there is no live tracking in Release 1.

:::

## Could not deliver

If a delivery fails, **a reason is required** — `BR-EXE-001` will not let the stop close
without one. You are asked at the moment you can actually answer.

| Reason | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer location closed** | Nobody there |
| **Customer refused the delivery** | They would not take it |
| **Goods damaged in transit** | It arrived unusable |
| **Access to the location blocked** | You could not reach the dock |
| **Ran out of time on the route** | The day ended first |
| **Vehicle issue** | The vehicle stopped you |
| **Rescheduled at the customer's request** | They asked for another day |

## What you cannot see, and why

| You cannot reach | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Other drivers' trips | You see only trips assigned to you |
| Demands, shipments, plans | Not part of your work |
| Warehouse readiness, loading tasks | Not part of your work |
| Inventory transfers | You would be able to read every movement in the company |
| Governance and reports | Not part of your work |

This is enforced by record rules on your account, not by hiding a menu. Full explanation:
[Driver Security Model](../execution/driver-security-model.mdx).

## If something is wrong

| Symptom | Check |
| --- | --- |
| **My Trips** is empty | No trip is assigned to you, or your trips are not released yet. Ask dispatch |
| **Depart** is not offered | Loading is not finished — a loading task is still open |
| **Delivered** is refused | The Odoo transfer has not been validated yet |
| Coordinates were not captured | The site is on plain `http`. Signature and photographs still work |
| A stop shows zero quantities | The transfer has not been validated. Do **not** record a failure for this reason |
