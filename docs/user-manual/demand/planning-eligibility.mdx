**Role**
Planner

**Navigate to**
`SCOP → Operations → Demands` → open the demand

## Purpose

Only **eligible** demand enters the daily planning process. This page explains the two
gates that get a demand there, and what each one actually reads.

The reason the checks are split is that they answer different questions:

| Gate | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Validate** | Are we *allowed* to move these goods? |
| **Mark Eligible** | Are these goods *available* to move? |

A demand can be perfectly available and still not be allowed — that is `BR-INV-002`. It can
be perfectly allowed and not available — that is readiness.

## Step 1 — Validate

**Before you start**
The demand is in **Submitted**.

**What to do**
Press **Validate**.

**What SCOP does**

1. **Re-derives ownership** on every line, from the five approved sources.
2. Evaluates the gate `demand_validate`, which runs `BR-INV-002`.
3. Moves the demand to **Validated**.

:::info[Ownership is re-derived, not merely re-checked]

At generation time the Odoo transfer often has no move lines yet, so there was nothing to
read an owner from — almost every outbound demand starts unresolved.

By the time you validate, the transfer is usually reserved and the owner is available. That
re-derivation is what turns "unresolved" from a permanent verdict into a transient one, and
it is why pressing **Validate** frequently succeeds on a demand that was showing a red
ownership flag five minutes earlier.

:::

**Expected result**
The demand shows **Validated**, and the ownership banner is gone.

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/06-demand-ownership-resolved.png"
  alt="SCOP Demand form after ownership resolution, showing the owner and ownership source populated on each line."
  caption="Ownership resolved. Every line now names an owner and how it was established."
/>

**If it refuses**

`BR-INV-002` names the lines whose owner could not be established.

| Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| The Odoo transfer is not reserved yet | Reserve it, then press **Resolve Ownership** and **Validate** again |
| The goods are consignment stock from an unflagged partner | Flag the partner — [Onboarding and Migration](../configuration/onboarding-and-migration.mdx) |
| No approved policy covers this source | See [Correcting Ownership](../ownership/correcting-ownership.mdx) |
| You lack Odoo **Inventory → User** | Ownership derivation reads Odoo's reservations. See [Permissions Reference](../roles/permissions-reference.mdx) |

## Step 2 — Mark Eligible

**Before you start**
The demand is in **Validated**, and the warehouse has done something with the goods.

**What to do**
Press **Mark Eligible**.

**What SCOP does**

1. Evaluates the gate `demand_eligible`.
2. **Refreshes allocation** against Odoo's current reservations.
3. Moves the demand to **Eligible for Planning**.
4. **Forms the shipment** — see below.

**Expected result**
The demand shows **Eligible for Planning**, its **Allocations** tab is populated, and a
shipment now exists on the **Shipments** tab.

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/07-demand-eligible.png"
  alt="SCOP Demand form in state Eligible for Planning."
  caption="Eligible for Planning. The shipment was formed at the same moment."
/>

## What eligibility checks

The gate requires readiness to be **Ready**, **Conditionally Ready** or **Partially Ready**.

| Readiness | Can the demand become eligible? | Can its shipment be planned? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ready** | Yes | **Yes** |
| **Conditionally Ready** | Yes | **Yes** |
| **Partially Ready** | Yes | **No** |
| **Not Ready** | No | No |
| **Blocked** | No | No |

:::warning[Eligible does not always mean plannable]

**Partially Ready** lets a demand become eligible but does **not** make its shipment
plannable. The requirement is acknowledged as real and available in part; the transport
requirement still cannot be put on a vehicle.

A demand that sits at **Eligible for Planning** and never appears in a plan is very often
this. Check the shipment's readiness, not the demand's state.

:::

## Allocation

The **Allocations** tab shows which specific stock is set aside for this demand.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Product** | What is allocated |
| **Location** | Where it physically is |
| **Lot** | Which batch, where tracked |
| **Owner** | Who owns it |
| **Allocated Quantity** | How much |

Allocation is a **projection**, refreshed at two moments only:

| Refreshed when | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Validate** | Ownership derivation reads the same evidence |
| **Mark Eligible** | This is the moment availability is asserted |

:::note[Allocation and readiness read different things]

Allocation is refreshed **at gates**. Readiness reads Odoo's reservations **now**.

That is deliberate: if readiness asked the projection, it would be measuring when the
projection last ran rather than what is in the warehouse. If you change a reservation in
Odoo, readiness moves immediately; allocation does not until the next gate.

:::

You can also read allocations across all demands at `SCOP → Operations → Allocations`.

## Shipment formation

Marking a demand eligible **forms its shipment automatically**.

```text
Validated Demand  →  Allocate Inventory  →  Create Shipment
                     (Mark Eligible does both)
```

A second requirement on the **same route and the same required date** joins the shipment
already open, rather than forming another. A shipment already **planned** onto a trip is
never joined — its capacity was sized when the plan was approved.

:::info[Create Shipment is a re-run, not a normal step]

**Create Shipment** stays on the eligible demand for two exceptional cases: a demand made
eligible before this behaviour shipped, and a demand whose shipment was cancelled.

Pressing it on a normal demand returns the shipment that already exists. It never creates a
duplicate.

:::

→ [Formation and Consolidation](../shipments/formation-and-consolidation.mdx)

## Verify

| Check | Where |
| --- | --- |
| The demand is **Eligible for Planning** | The demand's status bar |
| A shipment exists | The demand's **Shipments** tab |
| Allocation is populated | The demand's **Allocations** tab |
| The shipment is plannable | `SCOP → Operations → Warehouse Readiness` |

## Business rules

| Rule | Fires at | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BR-INV-002` | `demand_validate` | Blocking. Ownership must be resolved on every line |

The eligibility gate itself carries no rule in Release 1; its check is the readiness
condition described above.

## If it does not work

| Symptom | Check |
| --- | --- |
| **Validate** refuses with an ownership message | [Inventory Ownership](../ownership/inventory-ownership.mdx) |
| **Validate** raises an access error | You need Odoo **Inventory → User** |
| **Mark Eligible** refuses | Readiness is **Not Ready** or **Blocked** |
| Readiness is **Blocked** | Ownership unresolved on a shipment line, or every transfer cancelled |
| Eligible, but never planned | Readiness is probably **Partially Ready** — ask the warehouse to stage |
| No shipment after Mark Eligible | Unusual. See [Readiness and Shipment Problems](../troubleshooting/readiness-and-shipment-problems.mdx) |
| Allocations tab is empty | Nothing is reserved in Odoo for these lines |
