## When to use this

The driver is at the customer's door and cannot complete the delivery — the branch is closed, the customer refuses the goods, the loading bay is blocked, the goods were damaged in transit.

## The walkthrough

**Role**
Driver

**Navigate to**
`SCOP → My Work → My Stops` → open the stop

**What to do**
Tap **Could not deliver**.

**Expected result on a first attempt: refusal.**

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/34-failed-delivery-needs-reason.png"
  alt="Odoo warning dialog stating that the reason why the stop failed must be recorded before it can be closed."
  caption="The refusal, verbatim: “Record why stop 1 failed before closing it.”"
/>

:::danger[BR-EXE-001 — a failure must say why]

Anything other than a clean, complete delivery must record an **outcome reason**.

There is no version of *"the customer refused the goods and we did not record why"* that is acceptable. So this is a hard constraint rather than a warning: it holds in every state and admits no middle answer.

It applies to **partial** deliveries too, not only outright failures. A stop that delivered less than planned needs a reason just as much as one that delivered nothing.

:::

Notice where the refusal is raised. SCOP asks for the reason **at the moment the driver presses the button**, while they are standing at the customer's door and know the answer — not later, in a validation message naming a field they never saw.

## Choose the reason

**What to do**
Set **Outcome Reason** in the **What happened** section, then tap **Could not deliver** again.

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/35-failed-delivery-reasons.png"
  alt="SCOP driver stop form with the Outcome Reason dropdown open, listing the seven governed stop outcome reasons."
  caption="The governed reason list. These are the actual values in the system — a driver picks one, they do not type prose."
/>

| Code | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| `customer_closed` | Customer location closed |
| `customer_refused` | Customer refused the delivery |
| `goods_damaged` | Goods damaged in transit |
| `access_blocked` | Access to the location blocked |
| `time_exhausted` | Ran out of time on the route |
| `vehicle_issue` | Vehicle issue |
| `rescheduled_by_customer` | Rescheduled at the customer's request |

:::info

The list is governed, not free text, because reports group by it. A reason typed differently by every driver cannot be counted, and a reason that cannot be counted cannot be fixed.

:::

**What SCOP does**

- Sets the stop's **Status** to **Failed** and its **Outcome** to the derived value — `Failed`, `Partially Completed`, `Skipped`, or `Rescheduled`.
- Records the reason against the stop, where reporting and any resulting Exception can read it.

**Expected result**
The stop closes as **Failed**, carrying the reason.

**Verify**

- The stop shows **Outcome** and **Outcome Reason**.
- `SCOP → Operations → Exceptions` — an operational problem that needs follow-up is tracked here, with an owner and an ageing clock.
- `SCOP → Reporting → OTIF` — the delivery is counted as not in full.

## Status and outcome are two different things

This distinction matters when reading a stop:

| Field | Question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | How far did the stop get? `Planned → Arrived → Servicing → Completed / Failed / Skipped` |
| **Outcome** | What happened? `Completed`, `Partially Completed`, `Failed`, `Skipped`, `Rescheduled` |

A stop can reach the end of its journey and still have gone badly. Keeping the two apart is what lets *"we got there and it did not work"* be told apart from *"we never got there"*.

## Partial delivery

A short delivery is not recorded on the stop. It is recorded in Odoo:

1. The warehouse sets the actually delivered quantity on the Odoo transfer and validates it.
2. SCOP reads the quantities back onto the stop.
3. Because actual is less than planned, SCOP derives **Partially Completed** and requires an outcome reason.

See [Step 7 — Driver Delivery](../walkthrough/driver-delivery.mdx) for the full sequence and why the order matters.

## What to check if it does not work

| Symptom | Check |
| --- | --- |
| The refusal appears even for a good delivery | The Odoo transfer is not validated yet, so actual quantities are still zero |
| The reason list is empty | Reasons are seeded in `SCOP → Governance → Reason Codes` under the stop-outcome category |
| The reason cannot be changed afterwards | Correct — once a stop is closed its reason is part of the record |
