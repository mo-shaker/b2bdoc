## Step 2a — Verify the Demand

**Role**
Planner / Dispatcher

**Navigate to**
`SCOP → Operations → Demands`

**What to do**
Find the demand for the order you just confirmed. It is easiest to search by customer.

**Expected result**
A demand exists, in state **Draft**, referencing your delivery order.

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/03-scop-demand-list.png"
  alt="SCOP Demands list showing DMD-2026-000001 for Wood Corner in the Draft state."
  caption="SCOP created DMD-2026-000001 by itself when the delivery order was confirmed."
/>

**Verify**
Open the demand and check the fields below.

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/04-scop-demand-form.png"
  alt="SCOP Demand form for DMD-2026-000001 showing the lifecycle status bar, demand type, customer, and demand lines."
  caption="The Demand form. The status bar runs Draft → Submitted → Validated → Eligible for Planning → Planned → Released → In Execution → Completed."
/>

**1 — Reference**
`DMD-<year>-<number>`. Allocated by SCOP, stable for the life of the demand.

**2 — Demand type**
`Delivery` for an outbound customer movement, `Inbound` for a supplier receipt, plus `Collection`, `Transfer` and `Return`.

**3 — Customer and destination**
The partner, and the SCOP planning node the goods must reach.

**4 — Status bar**
The lifecycle. Only a demand at **Eligible for Planning** enters the daily planning process.

**5 — Demand lines**
Product, quantity requested, quantity fulfilled — and **Owner** with **Ownership Source**, which is the subject of the rest of this page.

## Step 2b — Walk the demand forward

**What to do**

1. Click **Submit**. The demand moves to **Submitted**.
2. Click **Validate**.

**Expected result on a first attempt: refusal.**

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/05-ownership-block-validate.png"
  alt="Odoo warning dialog stating that the action is blocked by BR-INV-002 because ownership source is missing on three products."
  caption="SCOP refuses to validate the demand, names the rule, and lists the exact products whose owner it cannot establish."
/>

## Why SCOP blocks this

:::danger[BR-INV-002 — ownership must be resolved before planning]

SCOP must know **who owns the goods** before it will let the movement be planned.

This is not bureaucracy. The company's warehouses hold consignment stock — goods physically present but owned by someone else. Moving goods on someone else's behalf without establishing whose they are is a legal statement, not a data-quality nicety. So SCOP declines to guess.

:::

For an **outbound** movement SCOP will *never* fall back to "presumably ours". It reads the owner recorded against the stock itself. If the warehouse has not recorded an owner, the answer is *unresolved* and the demand stops here — visibly, on a record you can open and fix.

Inbound receipts behave differently: goods the company bought are company-owned by approved policy, unless the supplier is a flagged consignment owner.

## How to resolve it

**Role**
Warehouse / Administrator

Ownership comes from the stock, so it is fixed in the stock:

1. Record the owner on the inventory. For a first-time setup use
   `SCOP → Master Data → Onboarding → 2 · Inventory Ownership`, which imports ownership from documented fact rather than assuming it.
2. Re-reserve the delivery order so the reservation picks up the owner.
3. On the demand, click **Resolve Ownership**.

**Expected result**

Each demand line now shows an **Owner** and an **Ownership Source** of `stock_move_line` — meaning the answer came from the recorded stock, not from a default.

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/06-demand-ownership-resolved.png"
  alt="SCOP Demand form with each line showing an owner and the ownership source stock_move_line."
  caption="Ownership resolved from the warehouse's own records. The Resolve Ownership button disappears once there is nothing left to resolve."
/>

**Now walk it forward again**

1. Click **Validate** — the demand moves to **Validated**.
2. Click **Mark Eligible** — the demand moves to **Eligible for Planning**.

<Screenshot
  src="quick-start/07-demand-eligible.png"
  alt="SCOP Demand form with the status bar highlighting Eligible for Planning."
  caption="Eligible for Planning. Only demand in this state enters the daily plan."
/>

**Verify**
The status bar shows **Eligible for Planning**.

**If it does not work**

| Symptom | Check |
| --- | --- |
| `BR-INV-002` still refuses | The reservation was not refreshed after the owner was recorded, so the move lines still carry no owner |
| Ownership is half-recorded | An owner and its source are set together or not at all; SCOP rejects one without the other |
| Lines disagree | Two moves on one line resolved to different owners. SCOP leaves this unresolved deliberately — it is a data problem for a person to look at |
| `Access Error` on `Stock Move` | Your user needs the Odoo **Inventory → User** group as well as its SCOP role |
